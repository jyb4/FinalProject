Baseball Terms Explained

Your comprehensive glossary of baseball terminology, from basic to advanced statistics.

At Bat (AB)

A player's turn to bat, resulting in a hit, walk, strikeout, or out.

Ball

A pitch thrown outside the strike zone that the batter doesn't swing at.

Earned Run Average

Average number of earned runs a pitcher allows per nine innings.

WAR (Wins Above Replacement)

Overall player value compared to a replacement-level player.
