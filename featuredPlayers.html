Featured Players

Babe Ruth "The Sultan Swat"

Babe Ruth revolutionized baseball with his power hitting. Originally a pitcher, he transformed the game by proving that home runs could be a consistent offensive weapon. His larger-than-life personality made him baseball's first superstar.

Major Achievements
  • 7× World Series champion
  • AL MVP (1923)
  • 12× AL home run leader
  • Baseball Hall of Fame (1936)
Dereck Jeter "The Captain"

Derek Jeter embodied leadership and clutch performance throughout his 20-year career with the Yankees. Known for his professionalism and consistency, he became the face of baseball in the 2000s and retired as one of the most respected players in history.

Major Achievements
  • 5× World Series champion
  • 14× All-Star
  • 5× Gold Glove winner
  • Rookie of the Year (1996)
