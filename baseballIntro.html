Introduction To Baseball

Master the fundamentals of America's pastime with our comprehensive beginner's guide.

Understanding The Game

The Objective

Baseball is played between two teams of nine players each. The goal is to score more runs than the opposing team by hitting a ball and running around four bases arranged in a diamond shape. A run is scored when a player successfully touches all four bases and returns to home plate.

How The Game Works

A baseball game consists of nine innings. Each inning has two halves - the top (visiting team bats) and bottom (home team bats). Teams alternate between batting and fielding. The batting team tries to score runs while the fielding team tries to get three outs to end the half-inning.

Basic Rules

A batter gets three strikes before they're out. Four balls (pitches outside the strike zone) result in a walk to first base. Batters can hit the ball into fair territory and run to bases. Fielders catch fly balls for outs or throw to bases before runners arrive. Three outs end the team's turn at bat.

Winnig the Game

The team with the most runs after nine innings wins. If tied after nine innings, extra innings are played until one team has more runs at the end of a complete inning. Unlike many sports, there's no game clock - games end when all innings are complete.
