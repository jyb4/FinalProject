MLB Teams

Baltimore Orioles

City: Baltimore, MD

Stadium: Oriole Park at Camden Yards

Founded: 1901

Fun Fact: Camden Yards revolutionized ballpark design when it opened in 1992

New York Yankees

City: New York, NY

Stadium: Yankee Stadium

Founded: 1901

Fun Fact: The Yankees have won 27 World Series, more than any other team

Tampa Bay Rays

City: St. Petersburg, FL

Stadium: Tropicana Field

Founded: 1998

Fun Fact: Only team to play in a domed stadium with artificial turf
